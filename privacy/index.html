Habits — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Habits is built so your health data stays yours. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of ours.

What Habits stores

What you log and set up: meals, water, weigh-ins, workouts, your meal and workout plans, goals, your focus plan, and a daily summary used for streaks and your weekly recap.

Where it’s kept

On your iPhone and, if you’re signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database so your other devices and Apple Watch stay in sync. Only you can see your private iCloud data — the developer can’t.

Apple Health

Habits only asks for what your chosen habits need. It reads steps, sleep, workouts, heart rate, water and weight to show your progress, and saves the water, meals, weigh-ins and workouts you log in Habits so they appear in Health too. You can change access any time in the Health app. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Coaching and food estimates

Tips, recaps and calorie estimates are worked out on your iPhone, using Apple Intelligence where available. Nothing is sent to an AI service. Food values come from the USDA FoodData Central database built into the app.

What Habits never does

No accounts or sign-ups. No ads, tracking or analytics. No selling or sharing of data. No servers of ours — the app works with no internet connection at all.

Not medical advice

Habits is for general wellness. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t give medical advice — talk to your doctor before big changes to your diet, exercise or weight, or if you have a health condition.

Feedback

If you tap Send feedback, your mail app opens with your app version, iOS version and device model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and no health data is included.

Your choices

Export everything as CSV files from Settings → Your data. Delete it all from the same place — from your iPhone, your iCloud and, if you choose, what Habits saved to Apple Health.

Children

Habits isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app.
